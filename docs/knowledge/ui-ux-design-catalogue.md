## 1. Principles of Design Selection

Design is not mere visual ornamentation; it is the structural communication of intent, hierarchy, and function.
1. **Context-First**: Enterprise dashboards demand high information density, calm neutrals, and tabular clarity; consumer applications require generous touch targets, fluid transitions, and tactile feedback.
2. **Anti-Slop Restraint**: Never combine multiple unmotivated aesthetics (e.g. purple gradient glow + translucent glass panels + neon badges).
3. **Hierarchy of Truth**: Visual styles are mutually exclusive visual directions. Layout patterns, interaction models, and accessibility rules are cross-cutting engineering dimensions.

## 4. Design-Skill Precedence Rules

To avoid design conflicts when multiple design skills are available, enforce this strict execution order:
1. **Product Brief & Brand Constraints**: User-approved decisions in `PROJECT.md` are authoritative.
2. **Design-System Extraction** (`design-md-chrome`): Extract tokens from provided brand references.
3. **Art Direction Authority** (`taste-skill`): Enforce purpose, density, and zero generic AI slop.
4. **Component Architecture** (`shadcn/ui` / `frontend-design`): Implement clean, accessible primitives.
5. **Typography Standards** (`typography-skills`): Apply proportional scale and line-length limits.
6. **Critique & Visual Audit** (`hallmark` / `impeccable`): Inspect for defects, inconsistency, and slop.
7. **Accessibility Verification** (`a11y`): Mandate keyboard, contrast, and reduced-motion compliance.
