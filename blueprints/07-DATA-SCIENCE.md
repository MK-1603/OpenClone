# Blueprint 07: Data Science & Statistical Analysis

**Project Family:** `data_engineering_and_data_science`  
**Primary Project Type:** `data_science_study`  
**Maturity Status:** `IMPLEMENTED`  
**Complexity Level:** `MEDIUM` to `RESEARCH_EXPERIMENTAL`  
**Default Delivery Targets:** `web_browser`, `containers_cloud`

---

## 1. Goals & Scope
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical testing, hypothesis evaluation, scientific reporting, and business intelligence.
- Emphasizes statistical rigor, confidence intervals, p-value interpretation, sampling bias detection, reproducible notebooks, and data visualizations.

## 2. Classification Rules
- Matched when the goal is insight extraction, statistical validation, or data storytelling rather than predictive model deployment or real-time pipelines.

## 3. Intake Questions & Required Information
1. Core business or research question and null hypothesis.
2. Dataset provenance, sampling methodology, missing values, and potential confounders.
3. Key metrics, statistical tests (t-test, ANOVA, chi-square, regression), and significance thresholds (alpha).
4. Deliverable format: Jupyter notebook, interactive dashboard (Streamlit), or executive report.

## 4. Domain & Capability Mapping
- **Mandatory Domains:** `01-SPECIFICATION`, `03-DATA-INTEGRITY`, `10-TESTING-AND-QA`, `18-DOCUMENTATION`.
- **Mandatory Engines:** `E-001`, `E-003`, `E-010`, `E-018`.

## 5. Specialist Workforce
- `data-scientist`: Statistical analysis, hypothesis formulation, and modeling.
- `data-analyst`: Exploratory data analysis, SQL queries, and visual reporting.
- `statistician`: Rigorous experimental design and bias detection.

## 6. Verification & Delivery Artifacts
- Reproducible Jupyter notebook executing end-to-end with fixed seeds.
- Statistical significance report documenting effect sizes and confidence intervals.
- Data dictionary and limitation disclosures.
