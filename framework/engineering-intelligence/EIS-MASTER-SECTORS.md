| **EIS-15** | **Software Product & Experience Engineering** | UI/UX engineering, interaction design, design systems, accessibility (WCAG 2.2), responsive design, user journeys |
| **EIS-16** | **Software Domain & Industry Engineering** | FinTech, HealthTech, EdTech, E-commerce, SaaS, Enterprise ERP/CRM, GovTech, legal compliance software |
| **EIS-17** | **Engineering Research, Operations & Lifecycle** | Technical research, literature reviews, benchmarking, FinOps, tech debt management, engineering economics |
