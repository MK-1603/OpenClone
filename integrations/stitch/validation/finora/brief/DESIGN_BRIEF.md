# Design Intelligence Brief: Finora Wealth Management

## 1. Product Context
- **Category:** Financial Technology & Wealth Management
- **Target Users:** Professional practitioners operating within financial technology & wealth management
- **Information Density:** ultra-compact
- **Navigation Architecture:** contextual-rail

## 2. Core Goals & Workflows
### Main Goals
- Rapidly achieve operational tasks in Financial Technology & Wealth Management
- Maintain clear situational awareness with zero latency

### Critical Workflows
- Intake & primary task execution
- Deep inspection and record mutation
- Reporting, audit, and verification

## 3. Action Hierarchy
- **Primary Actions:** Execute Trade, Transfer Capital, Rebalance Holdings
- **Secondary Actions:** Export Tax Statement, Set Volatility Alert, Switch Base Currency

## 4. Presentation & Responsiveness
- **Data Presentation Strategy:** tabular-ledger
- **Responsive Rules:** Desktop-first command architecture adapting smoothly to compact tablet drawers and vertical mobile stacks.

## 5. Archetype Alignment
- **Recommended Directions:** command_center, data_exploration, transaction_interface
- **Rejected Archetypes:**
- **visual_discovery**: Financial data demands rigorous tabular accuracy, not media masonry.
- **editorial_interface**: Real-time market tickers clash with relaxed literary spacing.

## 6. Constraints & Anti-Slop Guardrails
- Reject generic 4-card KPI top rows unless mathematically justified by distinct user workflows
- Strictly eliminate decorative purple-indigo gradient blobs and unreadable low-contrast glassmorphism
- Ensure full WCAG 2.2 AA contrast compliance and visible keyboard focus rings
